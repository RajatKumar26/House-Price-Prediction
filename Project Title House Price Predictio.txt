Project Title: House Price Prediction and Real Estate Market Analytics

1. Executive Summary:
This project focuses on transforming raw real estate data into actionable business insights and predictive intelligence. Following a structured business intelligence framework, the analysis helps stakeholders understand property valuation drivers and market trends.

2. Problem Statement:
Real estate buyers and sellers often struggle to determine fair property prices due to fluctuating market trends and unanalyzed property attributes. This project builds a predictive model to accurately estimate house prices based on historical data.

3. Business Intelligence Framework & Methodology:
* Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Essential metrics such as square footage, number of bedrooms, bathrooms, and overall house price.
* Trends & Drivers: Explaining how factors like property size and location grading directly influence valuation changes over time.
* Predictive Modeling: Utilizing Python libraries (Pandas, Scikit-Learn, and Linear Regression) to clean the dataset, perform exploratory data analysis, and train a price-prediction model.
* Recommended Actions: Providing strategic pricing guidelines for property sellers and investors based on predictive outcomes.

4. Conclusion:
The project successfully bridges raw data and strategic decision-making, offering a reliable framework to optimize real estate pricing strategies.
